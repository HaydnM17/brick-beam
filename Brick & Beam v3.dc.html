The shop

Named for the building. Built for the town

Brick & Beam is a coffee shop on King Street in downtown Beamsville, in a red brick heritage building with the brick left exposed inside. We opened in May 2026.

The name describes the room you are standing in. The town is in it too. Look for the gold lettering across from the Home Hardware.

Brick walls, walnut counter, good coffee
